References & evidence

Sources, Scope & Disclaimer

The guide synthesizes established anatomy, the Anatomy Trains model, trigger-point references, and emerging fascia research.

Evidence scope
Anatomy Trains — Myers, 4th Edition

Proposed myofascial-meridian framework used to organize line-based reasoning.

Wilke et al. (2016)

Metastudy examining anatomical continuity of proposed myofascial meridians.

Myofascial-release systematic reviews

Evidence summarized for chronic low-back pain and fibromyalgia, with attention to quality limitations.

Emerging fascia research

Hyaluronan, mechanotransduction, force transmission, and fascial-layer sliding.

Travell & Simons’ Trigger Point Manual

Established reference for trigger-point locations and referral maps.

Medical disclaimer: The Anatomy Trains model is a proposed clinical framework rather than universally established anatomical doctrine. This guide is for reference and education only and is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.
Image-use note: Every illustration on this page is an original, purpose-built artwork created for this guide in a single consistent clinical style, with all labels and annotations kept as live text so they remain accurate, searchable, and selectable. No third-party source images are reproduced, so the page is clear for public hosting.
